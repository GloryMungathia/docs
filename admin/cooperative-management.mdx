Cooperatives are the geographic and organizational hubs of the Pamodzi platform. Each cooperative represents a physical collection point where farmers bring their produce and agents carry out verification. Managing your cooperatives accurately ensures that demand campaigns can target the right locations and that buyers can plan logistics with confidence.

## What is a cooperative?

A cooperative is a registered hub with a name, address, province, and GPS coordinates. Farmers and cooperative agents are assigned to a cooperative — each user belongs to exactly one. When a wholesale buyer creates a demand campaign, they target a specific province; cooperatives in that province are the collection points that fulfill the campaign.

## Adding a cooperative

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to Cooperative Management">
    Select **Cooperative Management** from the dashboard sidebar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Add Cooperative">
    This opens the cooperative creation form.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the cooperative's details">
    Provide the cooperative name, physical address, phone number, and select the province from the list.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click Save to create the cooperative. It is now available for farmer and agent assignment and can be targeted by demand campaigns.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  GPS coordinates are set automatically when the assigned cooperative agent confirms the hub location from the mobile app. You do not need to enter coordinates manually.
</Tip>

## Provinces

Pamodzi operates across all ten provinces of Zambia. When creating a cooperative, select the province that matches its physical location:

- Central
- Copperbelt
- Eastern
- Luapula
- Lusaka
- Muchinga
- Northern
- North-Western
- Southern
- Western

## Viewing cooperative activity

Open any cooperative's detail page to see a complete picture of its activity:

- **Assigned farmers** — all farmers registered to this cooperative
- **Assigned agents** — the cooperative agents responsible for this hub
- **Active demand campaigns** — campaigns currently targeting this cooperative's province
- **Recent produce listings** — the latest produce submitted and verified at this hub

<Note>
  Each farmer and cooperative agent must be assigned to exactly one cooperative. Demand campaigns target a specific cooperative and province, so keeping assignments accurate is essential for correct campaign matching and fulfillment.
</Note>
