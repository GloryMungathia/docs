The Pamodzi admin dashboard is a web-based progressive web app (PWA) that gives administrators complete oversight of the platform. From a single browser window, you can manage users, cooperatives, demand campaigns, market prices, and financial transactions. Access it from any desktop browser at [phoenixdashboard.vercel.app](https://phoenixdashboard.vercel.app).

## Accessing the dashboard

Open your browser and go to **[https://phoenixdashboard.vercel.app](https://phoenixdashboard.vercel.app)**.

Log in with your admin email address and password. After submitting your credentials, you are required to complete MFA verification — a one-time PIN is sent to your registered phone number or email address. Enter the OTP to access the dashboard.

<Note>
  Admin accounts require MFA on every login. This is mandatory and cannot be disabled. Never share your admin credentials with anyone.
</Note>

## Security

All activity in the admin dashboard is logged and auditable. Every action you take — onboarding a user, archiving an account, updating cooperative details — is recorded against your administrator account.

<Warning>
  The admin dashboard has elevated permissions. All actions are logged and auditable. If you believe your account has been compromised, contact your system administrator immediately to revoke access.
</Warning>
