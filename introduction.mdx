Pamodzi is an agricultural ecosystem platform built for Zambia's produce markets. Its name means "together" in Bemba and Nyanja — a reflection of its core purpose. Farmers in rural Zambia often produce more than they can sell locally, yet lack a reliable channel to reach wholesale buyers who need large, consistent volumes. Pamodzi solves this coordination problem by giving every participant in the value chain — farmers, cooperative agents, and wholesale buyers — a purpose-built interface to work together in one shared marketplace.

## Platform Overview

Pamodzi organises its users into four roles, each with a dedicated interface and a distinct set of responsibilities:

- **Farmers** list their available produce using a USSD menu on any basic mobile phone. No smartphone or internet connection is required. Once a listing is verified and matched to a demand campaign, the farmer receives payment directly to their mobile money account.
- **Wholesale Buyers** launch *demand campaigns* — funded requests that specify exactly what crop, grade, quantity, and province they need. Funding is committed upfront, so farmers and agents know a real buyer is waiting.
- **Cooperative Agents** are field staff who work on behalf of a cooperative. They register farmers, enter produce details when a farmer cannot do so themselves, verify produce quality, and coordinate pickups at the cooperative hub.
- **Administrators** manage the platform through a secure web dashboard, overseeing users, cooperatives, campaigns, and finances.

## Key Features

  <Card title="USSD Produce Listing" icon="mobile">
    Farmers list crops directly from any basic feature phone using a simple menu — no internet, no smartphone, no app download required.

  <Card title="Demand Campaigns" icon="bullseye">
    Buyers define their exact requirements — crop type, grade, quantity, province, and moisture content — and fund the campaign before a single listing is collected.

  </Card>
  <Card title="Automated Payments" icon="money-bill-transfer">
    Once a buyer collects produce, Pamodzi automatically disburses payment from the platform merchant account to each farmer who contributed to the pool.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Market Price Reference" icon="chart-line">
    Buyers and agents can view current and historical commodity prices by province, giving everyone a shared, transparent price reference.
  </Card>
  <Card title="SMS Notifications" icon="message-sms">
    Farmers and buyers receive plain-language SMS updates at every key stage — listing confirmation, drop-off reminders, and campaign status changes.
  </Card>

<Note>
  Farmers do not need a smartphone or an internet connection to use Pamodzi. The entire farmer-facing experience runs over USSD — the same technology that powers mobile money menus — meaning any basic feature phone on a Zambian network is sufficient.
</Note>

## Glossary

The following terms appear throughout this documentation. Refer back here whenever you need a definition.

| Term | Definition |
|---|---|
| Demand Campaign | A wholesale buyer's funded request for a specific crop, grade, quantity, and province within a defined date window |
| Produce Pool | The aggregated set of farmer produce listings allocated against a single demand campaign |
| Produce Listing | A single farmer's submission of a quantity of a crop, which is either pending or already matched to a campaign |
| Cooperative | A regional hub that farmers belong to, represented by GPS coordinates and a province |
| Cooperative Agent | A staff member who manages field operations for a cooperative — verification, disbursal, and listing on behalf of farmers |
| USSD | Unstructured Supplementary Service Data — a protocol that lets feature phones interact with the platform via menu prompts, with no internet required |
| ZMW | Zambian Kwacha, the currency used for all transactions on Pamodzi |
| PACRA | Patents and Companies Registration Agency (Zambia) — wholesale buyers provide a PACRA number as proof of business registration |
| NRC | National Registration Card (Zambia) — used as a unique identity field when registering individual users on the platform |
