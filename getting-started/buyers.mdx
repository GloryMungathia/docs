Wholesale buyers use Pamodzi to create funded demand campaigns that specify exactly what produce they want — crop type, grade, quantity, and province. The platform aggregates matching farmer listings to fulfill your request, so you can source reliable volumes of produce directly from Zambian farmers without managing individual suppliers.

## Prerequisites

<Note>
  You'll need a valid company email address and your PACRA number — the registration number issued by Zambia's Patents and Companies Registration Agency — to complete sign-up.
</Note>

## How to Get Started

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign up">
    Download the Pamodzi mobile app and create your buyer account. Enter your company email address, choose a password, and provide your PACRA number. Tap **Create Account** to submit your registration.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify your identity with 2FA">
    After sign-up, Pamodzi uses two-factor authentication (2FA) to protect your account. When you log in with your email and password, the platform sends a one-time password (OTP) to your registered phone number. Enter that OTP code in the app to complete verification and receive your access token. Your session is active until you log out.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create a demand campaign">
    Tap **New Campaign** in the app. Fill in the details for your request:
    - **Crop type** — Maize, Sorghum, Groundnuts, or Millet
    - **Grade** — A, B, or C
    - **Target quantity** — in kilograms (kg)
    - **Province** — the region you want to source from
    - **Maximum moisture content** — as a percentage (%)
    - **Campaign date range** — the start and end dates for fulfillment

    Review all details carefully before proceeding — campaigns cannot be edited once funded.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fund your campaign">
    Provide your mobile money details and confirm the campaign amount. Payment is processed securely. Your campaign goes live and becomes visible to farmers as soon as funding is confirmed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Track fulfillment">
    Monitor aggregation progress in real time from the **Campaign** dashboard. You'll receive notifications as farmer produce is verified by cooperative agents and your campaign moves toward the target quantity.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Collect and complete">
    Once your campaign is fulfilled, you have a **72-hour window** to collect your produce from the cooperative hub. After collection is confirmed, payment is disbursed automatically to the farmers and the cooperative. The platform retains a small service fee.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Campaign States

Your campaigns move through the following states as they progress:

| State | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Aggregating** | Your campaign is live and farmer produce is being matched and pooled toward your target quantity. |
| **Fulfilled** | Your target quantity has been reached. You have a 72-hour window to collect from the cooperative hub. |
| **Cancelled** | The campaign was cancelled before fulfillment. Any funds held are returned. |
| **Archived** | The campaign is closed and no longer active. It remains visible in your campaign history for reference. |

## Supported Crops and Grades

Pamodzi currently supports demand campaigns for the following crops and grades:

| Crop | Available Grades |
|---|---|
| Maize | A, B, C |
| Sorghum | A, B, C |
| Groundnuts | A, B, C |
| Millet | A, B, C |

<Warning>
  Campaigns cannot be edited once funded. Ensure all details — crop type, grade, quantity, province, moisture content, and date range — are correct before confirming payment.
</Warning>

<Tip>
  You can view current market prices by province in the **Market Prices** tab before setting your campaign amount. This helps you offer competitive rates that attract more farmer listings.
</Tip>
