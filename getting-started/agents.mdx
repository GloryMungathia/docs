Cooperative agents are the field operators of the Pamodzi platform. You register farmers under your cooperative, verify produce quality when listings come in, confirm your hub's location, and manage the disbursement of payments to farmers once a buyer campaign is fulfilled. Your work connects the farmers in the field to the buyers on the platform.

## Prerequisites

You must be onboarded by your cooperative's administrator before you can access the platform. Your administrator will provide you with a staff ID and a temporary password to log in for the first time.

## How to Get Started

<Steps>
  <Step title="Receive your credentials">
    Your cooperative administrator provides your staff ID and initial temporary password. Keep these safe — you'll use your staff ID to log in to the Pamodzi app.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Download the mobile app">
    Install the Pamodzi app on your Android or iOS device. You can download it from the Google Play Store or the Apple App Store.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Log in">
    Open the app and select **Login as Cooperative Agent**. Enter your staff ID and password, then tap **Log In** to access your agent dashboard.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Register farmers">
    Use the **Farmer Registration** screen to add farmers under your cooperative. For each farmer you'll need:
    - Full name
    - Phone number
    - NRC number
    - Cooperative association

    Once registered, the farmer receives a confirmation SMS with their PIN and USSD instructions.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify produce">
    When farmers list produce via USSD, their listings appear in your **Verification Queue**. Inspect each listing in person and either approve or reject it based on grade, moisture content, and weight. Approved listings enter the produce pool for the matched buyer campaign; rejected listings are excluded.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm hub location">
    On your first use — or whenever your cooperative hub moves — confirm your hub's GPS coordinates using the app's location feature. This ensures buyers know the correct pickup point and the platform can route listings to your hub accurately.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Your Responsibilities

As a cooperative agent, your core duties on the platform are:

- **Registering farmers** — onboarding new farmers to your cooperative so they can list produce
- **Verifying produce listings** — inspecting and approving or rejecting farmer listings based on grade, moisture content, and weight
- **Managing disbursals** — coordinating payment disbursement to farmers after a buyer collects
- **Confirming hub GPS location** — keeping your cooperative's location up to date in the app

## Produce Verification

When you review a listing in your verification queue, you check three things: moisture content, weight, and grade. Each of these must meet the standard required by the active buyer campaign.

- If a listing passes all checks, tap **Approve** — the produce enters the pool and counts toward the campaign's target quantity.
- If a listing fails — for example, moisture content is too high or the grade doesn't match — tap **Reject** to exclude it. The farmer is notified by SMS.

<Note>
  Produce verification is irreversible. Once you approve or reject a listing, the decision cannot be undone. Double-check the grade and moisture content carefully before confirming.
</Note>

<Tip>
  You can register a farmer on their behalf if they are present with you but don't have access to a phone at that moment. Use the Farmer Registration screen the same way — just ensure you have their correct name, phone number, and NRC number.
</Tip>
