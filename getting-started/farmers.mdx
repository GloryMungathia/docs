Farmers use Pamodzi to list produce for sale through USSD on any mobile phone. You do not need a smartphone or internet connection — just a basic feature phone and a registered SIM card.

## Prerequisites

<Note>
  You need a registered mobile phone number and to be enrolled by a cooperative agent or admin. You cannot sign yourself up directly — your cooperative representative will register you on the platform.
</Note>

## How to Get Started

<Steps>
  <Step title="Get registered">
    A cooperative agent or admin registers you on the platform using your full name, phone number, and NRC number, and links you to your cooperative. Once registration is complete, you receive a confirmation SMS on your phone.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set your PIN">
    After registration, you receive a 4-digit PIN. This PIN is how you authenticate on the USSD menu. Keep it private and do not share it with anyone.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Dial the USSD code">
    Dial the Pamodzi USSD shortcode on your phone. You will see a menu appear on your screen with options to list your produce.
  </Step>
  <Step title="List your produce">
    From the USSD menu, select your crop type, enter the quantity you want to offer, and confirm your listing. Pamodzi will automatically match your listing against active demand campaigns from wholesale buyers.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Receive confirmation">
    Once your listing is submitted, you receive an SMS confirming it was recorded. You will continue to receive SMS updates as your produce is verified by your cooperative agent and aggregated into a buyer campaign.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What Happens Next

After you list your produce, a cooperative agent will inspect it and verify its quality — checking the grade, weight, and moisture content. If approved, your produce enters the pool for the matched buyer campaign. You will receive SMS notifications at each step: when your produce is verified, when it is aggregated, and when the campaign is fulfilled. Payment is disbursed to you and your cooperative automatically once the buyer collects.

## Changing Your PIN

You can change your 4-digit PIN at any time through the USSD menu. Simply dial the Pamodzi USSD shortcode, select the account or PIN option from the menu, and follow the prompts to set a new PIN. Choose a number you can remember but that others cannot easily guess.

<Tip>
  If you haven't received an SMS confirmation within a few minutes of listing your produce, contact your cooperative agent to confirm your registration is complete and your listing was received.
</Tip>

<Warning>
  Never share your PIN with anyone. Pamodzi staff will never ask you for your PIN.
</Warning>
