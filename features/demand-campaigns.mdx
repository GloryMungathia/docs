Demand campaigns are the core buying mechanism on Pamodzi. You create a campaign specifying your crop type, grade, quantity, and target province, fund it upfront via Flutterwave, and the platform automatically matches it against farmer produce listings — aggregating supply until your target quantity is reached.

## Creating a Campaign

When you create a demand campaign, you define exactly what you need. Every field below is required before your campaign can go live.

| Field | What to enter |
|---|---|
| **Crop type** | One of: Maize, Sorghum, Groundnuts, or Millet |
| **Grade** | A, B, or C — reflecting the quality tier you require |
| **Quantity** | Your target weight in kilograms |
| **Province** | One of Zambia's 10 provinces where produce should be sourced |
| **Moisture content** | The maximum acceptable moisture percentage for the crop |
| **Start date** | The date your campaign opens for farmer listings |
| **End date** | The date your campaign closes |
| **Amount** | The total ZMW value you are funding — charged via Flutterwave |

<Tip>
  Set a realistic date range to give farmers enough time to list produce. Most campaigns run 7–30 days for best results.
</Tip>

## Campaign Lifecycle

Your campaign moves through a series of states from creation to close. The table below explains what each state means for you.

| Status | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Pending** (no status) | Your campaign has been created but payment has not yet been confirmed |
| **Aggregating** | Your campaign is live; farmer listings are being matched and added to your produce pool |
| **Fulfilled** | Your target quantity has been reached; you will be notified to arrange collection |
| **Cancelled** | Your campaign ended before the target was reached |
| **Archived** | Your campaign has been closed and moved to your history |

## Funding Your Campaign

Before your campaign goes live, you fund it in full through Flutterwave's mobile money payment flow. Provide your mobile money details when prompted, and Flutterwave will process the charge to the Pamodzi merchant account. Once payment is confirmed, your campaign status moves from pending to **aggregating** automatically.

<Warning>
  Campaigns cannot be modified after funding. If any details need to change, cancel the current campaign and create a new one with the correct information.
</Warning>

## After Fulfillment

When your campaign is fulfilled, you receive a notification to arrange produce collection from the cooperative hub. You have a **72-hour window** from the fulfillment notification to collect. Payment is held securely in the platform merchant account during this window and is released to farmers and the cooperative only after collection is confirmed.

<Note>
  If you do not collect within the 72-hour window, contact your cooperative agent to arrange an extension before the deadline passes.
</Note>
