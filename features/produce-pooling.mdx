Produce pooling is how Pamodzi bridges individual farmer supply with wholesale buyer demand. When farmers list produce over USSD, the platform automatically checks for active demand campaigns that match on crop type, grade, and province — and groups qualifying listings into a single produce pool that counts toward that campaign's target quantity.

## How Pooling Works

You do not need to do anything to trigger pooling. The platform handles matching and aggregation automatically, step by step.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Farmer submits a listing">
    A farmer lists produce via USSD. The platform immediately checks for active demand campaigns that match on crop type, cooperative province, and grade criteria.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Listing is matched and added to the pool">
    If a matching campaign exists, the listing is linked to that campaign's produce pool. The pool's total allocated weight is updated to reflect the addition.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Cooperative agent verifies each listing">
    A cooperative agent reviews each listing in the pool, checking moisture content, weight, and grade. Listings that pass verification are marked **approved**; those that do not are marked **rejected** and removed from the pool.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Campaign is fulfilled">
    When the total verified weight in the pool reaches the campaign's target quantity, the campaign status moves to **fulfilled** and the buyer is notified to arrange collection.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Farmers do not need to search for campaigns manually. The platform matches listings automatically based on location, crop type, and grade.
</Note>

## What Farmers See

As your listing moves through the pooling process, you receive SMS notifications at each stage — when your listing is aggregated into a pool, when it is approved or rejected by the cooperative agent, and when the campaign is fulfilled. You do not need the mobile app or internet access to stay informed.

## What Buyers See

As a buyer, you can track aggregation progress in real time from the Campaign Details screen in the Pamodzi mobile app. The screen shows:

- Total aggregated weight vs. your campaign target
- Percentage of target reached
- Recent listing and verification activity

<Tip>
  Check the Campaign Details screen regularly during your campaign window to monitor progress and identify if your campaign needs a date extension.
</Tip>
