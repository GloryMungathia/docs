Pamodzi's USSD interface lets you list produce for sale using any basic mobile phone. The process takes less than 2 minutes with no internet connection required — just dial the Pamodzi shortcode and follow the on-screen menu prompts to submit your listing.

## What Is USSD?

USSD (Unstructured Supplementary Service Data) is a protocol that lets feature phones interact with services through simple menu prompts. Unlike mobile data or internet browsing, USSD works over the basic phone network — which means it works on any handset, in areas with standard network coverage, at no data cost.

## Listing Your Produce

Follow these steps to submit a produce listing from your registered mobile number.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Dial the Pamodzi shortcode">
    Dial the Pamodzi USSD shortcode from the mobile number you registered with your cooperative.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select 'List Produce'">
    Choose **List Produce** from the main menu options displayed on your screen.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your PIN">
    Enter your 4-digit PIN to verify your identity and access your account.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select your crop type">
    Choose your crop from the menu: Maize, Sorghum, Groundnuts, or Millet.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the quantity">
    Type in the quantity of produce you are listing, in kilograms.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm your listing">
    Review and confirm your listing. You will receive a confirmation SMS on your phone once the listing is submitted successfully.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Keep your phone charged and nearby after listing — a cooperative agent may contact you to arrange drop-off at the cooperative hub.
</Tip>

## Produce Listing States

After you submit your listing, it moves through the following states as it is matched, verified, and fulfilled.

| Status | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Not aggregated** | Your listing is live and being matched against active demand campaigns |
| **Aggregated** | Your listing has been assigned to a produce pool for a buyer's campaign |
| **Approved** | A cooperative agent has verified your produce and it passed quality checks |
| **Rejected** | Your produce did not pass quality verification and has been removed from the pool |
| **Archived** | Your listing has been closed and is no longer active |

<Note>
  Each listing is tied to your registered cooperative. Your produce will be matched against campaigns targeting your province — you do not need to search for campaigns yourself.
</Note>

## Getting Registered

Before you can list produce on Pamodzi, you must be registered on the platform. Registration is handled by your cooperative agent or a Pamodzi administrator — they will add your details and set up your account. Once registered, you receive your 4-digit PIN and can start listing produce immediately.
