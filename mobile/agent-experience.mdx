As a cooperative agent, the Pamodzi mobile app is your field operations tool. You register farmers, verify produce quality, and manage the logistics of produce collection and payment disbursement — all from your phone at the cooperative hub.

## Logging in

Cooperative agents use a separate login path from buyers. Your credentials are set up by your administrator before your first login.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Pamodzi app">
    Launch the app on your Android or iOS device.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tap Login as Cooperative Agent">
    This takes you to the agent-specific login screen.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your credentials">
    Type in your staff ID and password. These were provided to you by your platform administrator.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tap Login">
    Your agent home screen loads and you're ready to begin field operations.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Registering a farmer

You can register a farmer on their behalf directly from the app. The farmer does not need a smartphone — once you've submitted their details, they receive an SMS confirmation and can immediately list produce via USSD on their feature phone.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to the Farmer Registration screen">
    Tap the Farmer Registration tab in the bottom navigation.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the farmer's details">
    Fill in the farmer's full name, phone number, and NRC (National Registration Card) number. Confirm which cooperative they belong to.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set a 4-digit PIN for the farmer">
    Create an initial PIN the farmer will use to authenticate their USSD sessions. The farmer can change this PIN at any time through the USSD menu.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit">
    Tap Submit to complete registration. The farmer receives a confirmation SMS and their account is immediately active.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  You can register a farmer even if they're not physically present, as long as you have their correct NRC number and phone number.
</Tip>

## Verifying produce

Produce verification is one of your most important responsibilities as an agent. When a farmer lists produce against an active demand campaign, it enters your verification queue for review. Your approval determines whether that produce enters the campaign's produce pool.

To verify a listing:

1. Open your **verification queue** in the app.
2. Select a **pending listing** to review.
3. Record the actual **weight, grade, and moisture content** of the produce.
4. **Approve or reject** the listing based on your measurements.
   - **Approved** listings enter the produce pool and count toward the active demand campaign.
   - **Rejected** listings are excluded from the pool and the farmer is notified by SMS.

<Warning>
  Produce verification is final and cannot be reversed. Confirm measurements before approving or rejecting.
</Warning>

## Confirming hub location

Your cooperative's GPS coordinates are used by wholesale buyers to plan produce pickup and logistics. You confirm these coordinates directly from the app — Pamodzi uses your phone's location to set or update the hub's position. Keep this information accurate so buyers can reach your collection point without difficulty.

## Managing disbursals

Once a campaign is fulfilled and the wholesale buyer has collected the produce, you coordinate payment disbursement to the individual farmers who contributed. The app guides you through the disbursal process, showing each farmer's contribution and the corresponding payment amount. Confirm each disbursal to release funds to the farmer's registered mobile money account.
