The Pamodzi mobile app is your primary interface for sourcing produce as a wholesale buyer. From creating funded campaigns to tracking real-time aggregation, everything is available from your phone.

## Signing up

If you're new to Pamodzi, create your account directly from the app.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Download the Pamodzi app">
    Install the app on your Android or iOS device.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tap Create Account on the login screen">
    Select the wholesale buyer registration path on the opening screen.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your account details">
    Provide your company email, a password, your PACRA number, and your company name.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete 2FA verification">
    A one-time PIN (OTP) is sent to your registered email or phone. Enter it to confirm your identity.
  </Step>
  <Step title="You're in">
    Your Home Dashboard loads and your account is ready to use.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Home Dashboard

Your Home Dashboard gives you an immediate summary of platform activity. You'll see:

- **Campaign value** — the total ZMW value of your active campaigns
- **Aggregated mass** — total kilograms collected against your campaigns so far
- **Cooperative hub count** — the number of cooperatives contributing to your campaigns
- **Daily and weekly volume** — produce collected in the last 24 hours and the last 7 days
- **Aggregation progress** — a visual indicator of how close your campaigns are to fulfillment
- **Recent activity by province** — a feed of the latest produce movements across Zambia

## Creating a demand campaign

A demand campaign is how you source produce on Pamodzi. You define exactly what you want — crop, grade, quantity, province, and price — and fund it up front. Cooperative agents and farmers then aggregate produce against your campaign.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Tap New Campaign from the Campaigns tab">
    This opens the demand campaign form.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select a crop type">
    Choose from Maize, Sorghum, Groundnuts, or Millet.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set quality parameters">
    Choose a grade (A, B, or C) and enter the maximum acceptable moisture content for the produce.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter quantity and province">
    Set your target quantity in kilograms and select the Zambian province you want to source from.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set campaign dates">
    Choose the start and end dates for your campaign. Produce listings made during this window are eligible for aggregation.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter payment details">
    Enter the campaign amount in ZMW and provide your mobile money details for funding.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm and fund">
    Review all details and confirm. Your campaign goes live once payment is processed.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  Once a campaign is funded, the details cannot be changed. Review all fields carefully before confirming payment.
</Warning>

## Tracking your campaigns

The **Campaign List** shows all your campaigns in one place. Each campaign displays a status badge so you can see where things stand at a glance:

- **Aggregating** — produce is being collected against this campaign
- **Fulfilled** — your target quantity has been reached
- **Cancelled** — the campaign was cancelled before fulfillment

Tap any campaign to open its detail view, where you can see exactly how much produce has been collected, which cooperatives are contributing, and the timeline of recent activity.

## Market prices

The **Market Prices** tab shows current and historical commodity prices by province. Use it to understand how prices are moving across Zambia before you set your campaign amount.

<Tip>
  Set price alerts by watching market price trends before locking in your campaign amount.
</Tip>

## Profile and settings

Go to the **Profile** screen to update your account details, switch between light and dark themes, and log out. Your PACRA number and company name are visible here but cannot be edited — contact your administrator if these need to change.
