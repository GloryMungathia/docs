The Pamodzi mobile app is available for Android and iOS and provides role-specific interfaces for wholesale buyers and cooperative agents. Your experience in the app — the screens you see, the actions you can take — is determined entirely by your role on the platform.

## Who uses the mobile app

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Wholesale Buyers" icon="basket-shopping">
    Create and fund demand campaigns, track fulfillment progress, and monitor current market prices by province — all from your phone.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Cooperative Agents" icon="users">
    Register farmers on their behalf, verify produce quality at the hub, manage disbursals, and confirm your cooperative's GPS location for buyer logistics.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## App screens overview

The app includes the following main screens. Screens marked as agent-only are not visible to buyers, and vice versa.

- **Login** — separate login paths for wholesale buyers and cooperative agents
- **Home Dashboard** — an at-a-glance summary of campaign activity, aggregation progress, and recent activity by province
- **Demand Campaign** — create and fund a new produce request (buyers only)
- **Campaign List** — view all your campaigns and their current status (buyers only)
- **Campaign Details** — drill into the progress of a specific campaign (buyers only)
- **Market Prices** — current and historical commodity prices by province
- **Profile** — manage your account details, switch themes, and log out
- **Notifications** — platform alerts and status updates
- **Farmer Registration** — register a new farmer on their behalf (agents only)
- **Produce Verification** — review, approve, or reject pending produce listings (agents only)

## App navigation

The app uses bottom navigation tabs to move between its main sections. The tabs you see depend on your role — buyers see campaign and market price tabs, while agents see farmer registration and produce verification tabs. Tap any tab to switch between sections instantly.

<Tip>
  The app supports both light and dark themes. Switch between them in your Profile settings.
</Tip>
