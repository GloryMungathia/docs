## How a Trade Comes Together

Every transaction on Pamodzi follows the same straightforward path — from a buyer's funded request to a farmer's confirmed payout.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Buyer Creates a Demand Campaign">
    A wholesale buyer specifies the crop, grade, target quantity, province, and acceptable moisture content, then funds the campaign upfront. The campaign immediately opens for farmer listings.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Farmers List Their Produce">
    Farmers dial a USSD code on any basic mobile phone — no smartphone or internet needed — and submit how much of that crop they have available. Each submission is a produce listing.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Cooperative Agent Verifies Quality">
    A cooperative agent visits the farmer, confirms produce quality, approves or rejects each listing, and records the cooperative hub location. Only approved listings enter the produce pool.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Campaign Is Fulfilled and Payment Disbursed">
    Once the pool reaches the buyer's target quantity, the buyer is notified and arranges pickup. After collection, Pamodzi disburses payment to each contributing farmer, retaining a 5% platform fee.
  </Step>
</Steps>
